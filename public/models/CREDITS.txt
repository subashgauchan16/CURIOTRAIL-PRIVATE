island-high.glb and island-low.glb are based on "Viking Island - Minecraft"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/viking-island-minecraft-fd76e3824b7647d98be20d3e2160e018)
by cubical.xyz (https://sketchfab.com/cubical), licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: merged into chunks, baked to flat per-face colours (the original
texture atlas is not included), water and seabed removed, simplified,
quantized and meshopt-compressed. See tools/optimize-island.mjs.
These two files are licensed CC-BY-4.0, not under the CurioTrail licence.

island-b/VikingIsland_Scene.glb is the CURIOtrail Viking Island built in Blender
from original scripted geometry (see the Viking_refined island project). The
Sketchfab model above was consulted as a layout reference only; no geometry or
textures from it are included in this file.

characters/Pathfinder.glb, Mira.glb and Tove.glb are built in Blender
(tools/characters/build_character.py) from these Quaternius packs, all
CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
"Universal Base Characters", "Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy" and
"Universal Animation Library" (https://quaternius.com). Attribution is not
required by CC0; it is given here for provenance.

island-realistic/ (manifest.json, shared.glb, chunk_*.glb, instances.json,
opt_impostor_*.webp) is the streamed realistic Viking island. Its layout and
terrain heights are derived from "Viking Island - Minecraft" by cubical.xyz
(CC-BY-4.0, link above): the block heightfield, building footprints, walkways,
ship and tree positions were measured from that model and rebuilt as new
geometry. No mesh, texture or Minecraft block artwork from the original is
included. These files are therefore credited under CC-BY-4.0 as an adaptation.
Surface textures are CC0 1.0 from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com):
aerial_grass_rock, aerial_rocks_02, coast_sand_01, stony_dirt_path (baked into
the terrain tiles), brown_planks_05, medieval_wood, thatch_roof_angled,
mossy_stone_wall, knotted_pine_bark. Everything else (longhouses, towers, great
hall, boardwalks, longships, sails, shields, trees, rocks) was generated by the
project's own Blender scripts. Built by tools/island-pipeline
(npm run build-island-realistic).
