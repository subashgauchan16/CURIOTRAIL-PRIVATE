# Commercial licensing

> **Draft.** This page describes the *proposed* CurioTrail licence (see [LICENSE](../LICENSE)), which has not yet been legally reviewed. Nothing here is a binding offer or a legal guarantee. Items in [square brackets] are placeholders.

## Free for individual learners

Anyone can play the published game at https://denilthaks.github.io/CURIO/ for their own learning, free, with no account and no ads. That includes students, job-seekers and people learning for work. Teachers and trainers can point learners to the official game address without a licence.

## When an organisation needs a commercial licence

A written commercial licence is needed before an organisation (a company, school, training provider, charity or public body):

| Use | Licence needed? |
|---|---|
| An individual plays the official game to learn | No |
| A teacher shares the official game link with a class | No (proposed; under legal review) |
| A company runs CurioTrail as part of staff training | Yes |
| Hosting, embedding or mirroring the game on another site or platform | Yes |
| Adding it to a learning management system or product | Yes |
| White-labelling, rebranding or reselling | Yes |
| Building courses or products from its quests, lessons or code | Yes |
| Copying or redistributing the source code | Yes |

## What stays free whatever the licence says

Third-party material keeps its own licence (see [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md)). In particular, the island model files remain under CC-BY-4.0. Copies of CurioTrail published earlier under the MIT licence remain under MIT for those versions (see [IP-OWNERSHIP.md](IP-OWNERSHIP.md)).
