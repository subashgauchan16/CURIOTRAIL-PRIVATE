# Stage 1: character, Blender and asset audit

3 November 2026 · `CURIOTRAIL-PRIVATE` at `f0db667` · read-only. No source models, Blender files or repository code were changed.

**Result: Stage 1 stops here, as the brief requires, because of three constraints that must be decided before any character prototype.**
1. The island in the repository **is not the Blender-created island**.
2. The project's own art-direction candidate **rules out voxel**, yet the repository island is voxel.
3. There is **no production route** that can create "detailed stylised realism" characters to an approved design inside this project's tooling without new spending or an artist.

Details and options follow.

The brief says to *"use the existing Blender-created island and its current low-/high-detail GLB assets"*. Those two things describe **different islands**: the low/high GLBs are island A, and the Blender island is B. Their destinations, scale and layout differ. There is **no editable Blender source for island A**, only the third-party glTF. Preserving it means preserving the GLBs and the optimisation script, which this work will do.

## 4. Renderer constraints (measured in code)

| Aspect | Current | Effect on detailed characters |
|---|---|---|
| Island material | One `MeshLambertMaterial`, flat shading, vertex colours, foliage shimmer | Fine for voxels. Textured PBR characters will be lit differently |
| Lights | Hemisphere plus one directional light; **no shadows, no environment map, no tone mapping** | Leather, cloth and skin need an environment map (three's built-in `RoomEnvironment` + PMREM, no new dependency) and tone mapping; the island can opt out with `toneMapped: false` so its look doesn't change |
| Texture pipeline | None: the island has no textures | Character textures need a budget. Three 1024² sets (base, normal, ORM) per character ≈ 12 MB of GPU memory each uncompressed; KTX2 would need the Basis transcoder copied into `public/` (about 0.5 MB, from three's examples) |
| Geometry budget | Island 242k (low) / 1.09M (high) triangles | About 20–40k triangles per skinned character is affordable on desktop. Unmeasured on phones |
| Performance evidence | Software rendering only (0–3 fps in e2e) | **A real GPU measurement is required** before accepting any character budget |

**Conclusion on the renderer:** Three.js r186 can display detailed, textured, skinned characters at this scale. **The renderer is not the blocker.** The blockers are which island, a coherent art direction, and who makes the characters.

## 5. The constraint the brief asked me to report

1. **Style coherence.** The brief requires characters to *"complement the existing island"* and to have *"detailed stylized realism"*. The repository island is flat-colour Minecraft voxels, and the project's own art-direction candidate says to *avoid voxel*. Realistic characters on that island would look imported from another game, and changing the island is forbidden. Both requirements can't be met with island A as it is.
2. **No approved designs.** "Do not silently invent replacement character identities" can't be satisfied while the three Pathfinder designs conflict and Figma marks them unapproved.
3. **Production capacity.** Premium stylised characters need real modelling, sculpted faces, hair cards, cloth, UVs, textures, retopology, proper weighting and hand-keyed animation. I can script block-style rigged characters well, as I did in island B. I **can't** reliably produce characters of the quality shown in the Figma references by script, and I won't present a lower-quality result as meeting the brief. Possible routes:

| Route | What it involves | Quality realistically reachable | Cost and risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1: 3D artist** (commission one character to the Figma sheet, delivered as `.blend` + GLB) | Brief from Figma S1 plus frame 25; artist delivers rig, idle and walk | Matches the art direction | Money beyond the £50–£100 ceiling. Paying a contractor needs the 02a advice (business activity). Must be done under a written IP assignment |
| **R2: AI-generated mesh + Blender rigging** (the Blender MCP `generate_3d` from the Figma Pathfinder image; Rigify auto-weights; hand-fixed weights; walk and idle from a standard rig) | One prototype, validated in the renderer | Textures and silhouette often good; **faces, hands and hair weak**; deformation needs manual cleanup | Uses generation credits. Ownership and originality of AI output unresolved (02a C11). May fall short of "expressive faces" |
| **R3: open-licence base body (CC0) customised to the design** (e.g. a CC0 rigged base with animation library; clothes, hair and materials modelled on top in Blender) | Proper skeleton and reusable animations | Solid stylised, below "premium maquette" without an artist's sculpting | Needs a download (your permission per file); CC0 avoids attribution and IP issues |
| **R4: refine the block-style characters** (island B's rigged GLBs), with better faces (textures), layered clothing blocks and more bones | Fits *voxel* islands A and B | Coherent with the island, but **not** "detailed stylised realism" | Cheapest and fastest. **This is a change of art direction and needs your explicit decision**; I won't do it silently |

## 6. Decisions needed

1. **Island:** use **A** (repository island, voxel, already wired to quests) or **B** (the Blender-refined island, original geometry, 1 m scale, orchard and baskets, not yet in the repo)? Switching to B means re-mapping the 5 destination coordinates and replacing the island GLBs: a replacement the brief currently forbids, so it can only be your instruction.
2. **Art direction for this milestone:** characters that **match the island's block style** (R4), or **detailed stylised** characters (R1, R2 or R3), accepting a visible style mismatch on a voxel island until the island itself is re-art-directed in a later milestone?
3. **Canonical designs:** which Pathfinder (Figma frame 25 or the concept sheet), and which Mira and Tove references are final?
4. **Production route:** R1, R2, R3 or R4. R1 needs budget and 02a advice; R2 needs generation credits and acceptance of AI-asset ownership risk; R3 needs your permission to download a named CC0 asset.
5. **Carried over from Phase 0, still unanswered:** private `main` vs the public repo's newer Market Ledger; scope exclusions (jump, crouch, Zeus Band, interiors, joystick).

**Recommendation:** if the goal is an explorable vertical slice soon, pick **island A + R4 for now**: refined block characters, rigged, with idle and walk. Build movement, NPCs, HUD and the quest bridge with a **character-agnostic loader**, so detailed characters (R1 or R3) can replace the block ones later without code changes. Lock the art direction and island choice separately, because realistic characters only make sense once the island style matches them.

**Independent of these decisions:** the movement, collision, camera, interaction and HUD work (Phase 0 plan, steps 1–3 and 5–8) can proceed, since it only needs a GLB with a standard skeleton and `Idle` and `Walk` clips.
