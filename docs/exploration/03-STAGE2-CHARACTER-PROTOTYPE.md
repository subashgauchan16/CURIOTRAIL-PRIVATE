## 3. Honest quality assessment against the brief

| Brief asks for | What the prototype has | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Expressive faces | Textured stylised face with modelled eyes and eyebrows. **No facial rig, blinking or expressions** | Needs a face rig (shape keys or bones) or an artist |
| Convincing hair | Curly cap built from sculpted spheres. Reads as curls at play distance; **looks lumpy in close-up** (`07-face-closeup.png`) | Hair cards or a sculpt by an artist would be better |
| Layered Viking clothing | Shirt with a structured waist panel, strap (its edge is **stair-stepped in close-up**), satchel, boots. The shirt is a **recoloured fantasy bodice**, so the waist panel keeps lacing detail; Figma shows a plain chambray shirt | A modelled shirt would match better |
| Leather and fabric materials | Base colour, normal and roughness/ORM maps from the CC0 set | Adequate for stylised |
| Coherent proportions | The free tier only includes **"Superhero" proportions**, slightly heroic. The "Regular" bodies are in the paid source tier ($19.99) | Paid tier or an artist |
| Complements the island | **Does not fully.** The detailed character on block-built island B is a visible style mismatch, as you accepted | Island re-art-direction is a later milestone |

**Verdict:** "detailed stylised" game quality at indie level is achievable on this renderer and budget. Premium-maquette quality (Figma S1) is **not** reached by the CC0 route. The constraints are art production (faces, hair, a bespoke shirt, proportions), not the renderer.
